## 1. Swami Vivekananda

### Evergreen angles (each is one short)
- The Chicago speech: why "Sisters and Brothers of America" got a two-minute standing ovation.
- Narendranath Datta → Vivekananda: the question he asked Ramakrishna ("Have you seen God?").
- 3 days meditating on the rock at Kanyakumari (Dec 1892) — the origin of his mission.
- The 1893 ship voyage with Jamsetji Tata, Tata's 1898 letter to him, and the Indian Institute of Science story — frame it as "inspired", not "founded", and verify details before scripting.
- Four Yogas in 45 seconds: Karma, Bhakti, Jnana, Raja.
- Education as "manifestation of perfection already in man" — what that means for students.
- "Daridra Narayana" — service to the poor as worship; Ramakrishna Mission.
- Died at 39 (4 July 1902) — what he did in under a decade.
- Football and the Gita line ("You will be nearer to Heaven through football than through the study of the Gita") — it's genuine (Lectures from Colombo to Almora, CW Vol. 3 — check the exact lecture before citing it); explain the context: he meant strength of body and nerve, not dismissal of the Gita.

### Verified quotes (public domain — *Complete Works of Swami Vivekananda*, Advaita Ashrama)
Cite as "— Swami Vivekananda" on screen; put the source in the description.

| Quote | Source |
|---|---|
| "Sisters and Brothers of America" | Response to Welcome, Parliament of Religions, Chicago, 11 Sep 1893 (CW Vol. 1) |
| "Each soul is potentially divine. The goal is to manifest this Divinity within by controlling nature, external and internal." | *Raja Yoga* (CW Vol. 1) |
| "Take up one idea. Make that one idea your life — think of it, dream of it, live on that idea." | *Raja Yoga*, ch. "Pratyahara and Dharana" (CW Vol. 1) |
| "Arise, awake, and stop not till the goal is reached." | Lectures from Colombo to Almora (CW Vol. 3); his rendering of Katha Upanishad 1.3.14 *uttishthata jagrata prapya varan nibodhata* |
| "Education is the manifestation of the perfection already in man." | CW Vol. 4, writings on education |
| "Strength is life, weakness is death." | CW Vol. 2, "Practical Vedanta" lectures (verify part number before printing a citation) |
| "As different streams having their sources in different places all mingle their water in the sea, so, O Lord, the different paths which men take… all lead to Thee." | Chicago Response to Welcome (quoting the Shiva Mahimna Stotra), CW Vol. 1 |

When a quote isn't in this table, search the *Complete Works* text (freely available online) before using it. If you can't find it, don't attribute it.

### Commonly misattributed — do not use as his words
- "Talk to yourself once in a day, otherwise you may miss meeting an excellent person in this world."
- "In a day, when you don't come across any problems, you can be sure that you are travelling in a wrong path."
- Most image-macro quotes about love, relationships, money or "attitude" — they almost never appear in the *Complete Works*.
A good short in its own right: "3 quotes Vivekananda never said."

## 2. Da Ra Bendre (ದ. ರಾ. ಬೇಂದ್ರೆ)

Pen name *Ambikatanayadatta* (ಅಂಬಿಕಾತನಯದತ್ತ); called *Varakavi* (ವರಕವಿ, "gifted poet"). Dharwad, Sadhanakeri. Leading voice of the Navodaya movement; made the Dharwad spoken Kannada idiom into high poetry. Founded the *Geleyara Gumpu* (friends' circle) of writers in Dharwad.

**Copyright**: Bendre died 26 Oct 1981 → his works are under copyright in India until 1 Jan 2042. Quote at most a line or two, with credit, as part of commentary. Explain, paraphrase, translate the *idea* — don't recite whole poems. Many of his songs are also film/recorded music (e.g. sung by P. Kalinga Rao and others): don't use those recordings as background audio.

### Works (selection)
- *Gari* (ಗರಿ, 1932) — first major collection
- *Sakhigeeta* (ಸಖೀಗೀತ)
- *Nadaleele* (ನಾದಲೀಲೆ)
- *Aralu Marulu* (ಅರಳು ಮರಳು) — Sahitya Akademi Award (1958)
- *Naaku Tanti* (ನಾಕುತಂತಿ, "Four Strings") — Jnanpith Award (1973)
- Also Padma Shri (1968), Kelkar Prize, honorary doctorates.

### Poems/songs people know (good hooks — explain, don't reproduce)
- **"Naanu badavi, aata badava"** (ನಾನು ಬಡವಿ ಆತ ಬಡವ) — love that is rich in poverty.
- **"Yuga yugadi kaledaru, yugadi marali baratide"** (ಯುಗಯುಗಾದಿ ಕಳೆದರೂ…) — the Ugadi song; essential post around Ugadi.
- **"Belagu"** — the sunrise poem ("Moodala maneya muttina neerina…").
- **"Kurudu kaanchana"** (ಕುರುಡು ಕಾಂಚಾಣ) — gold/money as a blind, dancing force.
- **"Naaku Tanti"** — the four strings read as I, you, he/she, and the world/that which binds them; mystic and dense, great for "explained in 45 seconds".
- **"Shravana"** poems — the monsoon in Dharwad.

### Angles
- "Who was Varakavi Bendre?" in 45s (birth, Dharwad, Jnanpith, Navodaya).
- "Naaku Tanti explained" — what are the four strings?
- Bendre and Vivekananda/Aurobindo: Bendre was deeply influenced by Sri Aurobindo's thought (verify specific claims before scripting).
- Dharwad: the city of poets and musicians (Bendre, Gangubai Hangal, Bhimsen Joshi's Hindustani ties, Mallikarjun Mansur).
- The 8 Kannada Jnanpith winners — Kannada has the most Jnanpiths among Indian languages (Kuvempu, Bendre, Shivaram Karanth, Masti, V.K. Gokak, U.R. Ananthamurthy, Girish Karnad, Chandrashekhara Kambara).

## 4. Sensitive ground
Skip or handle with plain facts only:
- Party-political use of Vivekananda (all parties claim him). Don't take sides.
- Religious comparisons that rank faiths. Vivekananda's own message was acceptance of all paths — keep to that.
- Karnataka language/border disputes, caste claims about either figure, unverified "secret history" content.
- Health/medical claims from yoga or scripture.
