# Anniversary & national-day calendar

Machine-readable copy of this list lives in `scripts/fetch_trends.py` (`CALENDAR`). Keep both in sync when editing.
Lunar festivals (Diwali, Holi, Ugadi, Ganesh Chaturthi, Navaratri, Shivaratri) move every year — check the current year's date via web search before using them.

## Swami Vivekananda (12 Jan 1863 – 4 Jul 1902)
| Date | Occasion | Angle ideas |
|---|---|---|
| 12 Jan | Birth anniversary — **National Youth Day** (India, since 1985) | "Arise, awake…" for students; his advice on strength and character |
| 11 Sep | Address at the Parliament of the World's Religions, Chicago (1893) | "Sisters and Brothers of America"; universal acceptance |
| 4 Jul | Mahasamadhi at Belur Math | his last days; legacy in 39 years |
| 1 May | Ramakrishna Mission founded (1897) | service as worship |
| 25 Dec – 27 Dec | Meditation on the rock at Kanyakumari (1892) | the Vivekananda Rock Memorial story |

Tithi-based Vivekananda Jayanti (per Hindu calendar) also falls in Jan/Feb — check the year.

## Da Ra Bendre (31 Jan 1896 – 26 Oct 1981)
Dattatreya Ramachandra Bendre, pen name *Ambikatanayadatta*, "Varakavi". Born in Dharwad (Sadhanakeri).
| Date | Occasion | Angle ideas |
|---|---|---|
| 31 Jan | Bendre Jayanti | who was Varakavi; Sadhanakeri and Dharwad |
| 26 Oct | Death anniversary | his lines on time, life, love |
| — | Jnanpith Award for *Naaku Tanti* (1973, announced 1974) | what "Naaku Tanti" (four strings) means |
| — | Padma Shri (1968), Sahitya Akademi Award for *Aralu Marulu* (1958) | lesser-known honours |
| 1 Nov | Karnataka Rajyotsava | Kannada poets you should know — Bendre, Kuvempu, Masti… |
